# Source register

Every publisher source found for industry-standard diagrams: where it downloads from, its version and how a new one is detected, what it contains, its terms quoted from the publisher, and whether the converted pack may be committed. Downloads live in merrow's cache, never in a repo. Checked 2026-11-17: each recorded sha256 matched its download, and the Kubernetes and Cisco licence quotes were read in the source.

A Yes verdict still needs the attribution the licence asks for. Unclear is a legal call, not a default.
